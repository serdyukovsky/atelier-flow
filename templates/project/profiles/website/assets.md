# Website asset workflow

Treat visual assets as part of the product, not decoration added at the end.

## Asset inventory

For every important placement, record:

- purpose and section;
- source: user-provided, real product screenshot, generated, or licensed stock;
- aspect ratio and responsive crop;
- light/dark behavior;
- alt text or decorative status;
- license or attribution requirement;
- optimized output format and size budget.

## Priority

1. Real product screenshots or user-provided brand assets.
2. Purpose-generated assets that match the approved art direction.
3. Clearly licensed stock or temporary placeholders.

Never present a placeholder as a real customer, product, metric, or testimonial. Do not use random image URLs in a production path.

## Image quality

- Use meaningful filenames and dimensions.
- Provide responsive sizes and avoid shipping oversized originals.
- Reserve space to prevent layout shift.
- Use `alt` for informative images and empty alt for purely decorative images.
- Check focal points and crops on narrow screens.
- Generate Open Graph images deliberately; do not assume the hero crop works as a social preview.

## Deliverable

Before declaring the page complete, list missing assets and decide whether they block release or are acceptable as labeled placeholders.
